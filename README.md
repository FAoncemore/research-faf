# Research FAF

**Research & Tactical Enhancement Mod for Supreme Commander: Forged Alliance Forever (FAF)**

**Current Version: 12**

Inspired by the research system in StarCraft II, Research FAF introduces a research progression system into Supreme Commander.

### Core Gameplay

- Build Research Labs to generate research points.
- Earn additional research points by destroying enemy units.
- Spend research points to upgrade units.
- Unlock and deploy various field support abilities.

### Game Modes

Research FAF is an IMBA-style mod specially designed for AI players and survival maps.

### Configuration

Players can customize the initial research ability points:

`mods/Research FAF/lua/ui/InitialData.lua`

Modify `InitialAbility` to your preferred starting value.
