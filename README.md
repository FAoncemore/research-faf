# Research FAF

**A Research & Tactical Enhancement Mod for Supreme Commander: Forged Alliance Forever (FAF)**

Research FAF introduces a research progression system that rewards battlefield performance and research infrastructure. Players can earn research points, unlock enhancements, deploy support abilities and gain additional battlefield intelligence.

## Core Features

### 1. Research System
- Earn research progress through enemy mass destruction.
- Generate research progress through research labs.
- Track research progress through an in-game interface.
- Unlock and upgrade different abilities using accumulated research points.

### 2. Enhancements
Improve your army and economy through various research upgrades:

- Resource production
- Building speed
- Mobile unit speed
- Unit health and shields
- Rate of fire and weapon damage
- Weapon range and vision
- Veterancy-related enhancements

### 3. Research Categories

The research interface includes five main sections:

- **Enhancements** – Improve unit and economy performance.
- **Intel** – Access additional battlefield intelligence.
- **Field Supports** – Unlock tactical support abilities.
- **Weapons** – Access offensive and special weapon abilities.
- **Defender** – Defensive abilities and support options.

### 4. Special Abilities
Additional abilities include:
- Resource balance management
- Mass generation from kills
- Energy storage
- Reinforcement deployment

### 5. Battlefield Intelligence & Alerts
Research FAF also provides additional battlefield awareness:

- Experimental unit detection and destruction notifications.
- Nuclear weapon and strategic artillery alerts.
- Strategic threat monitoring, including Mavor, Paragon, Salvation, Scathis and Yolonaoss.
- Defender-loss notifications.
- Map-size visualization and countdown information.
- In-game health and status information.

## Installation

1. Open the FAF client.
2. Find Research FAF in the FAF Vault.
3. Install and enable the mod.
4. Launch a compatible game with the mod enabled.
